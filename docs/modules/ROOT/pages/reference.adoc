== Bibliography

The algorithms in xref:frames.adoc[], xref:gnss.adoc[], xref:lateration.adoc[], and xref:geodesic.adoc[] are based
on the sources below. Each page links back to the entries it uses.

[[bib-groves2013]]
Groves, P. D., _Principles of GNSS, Inertial, and Multi-sensor Integrated Navigation Systems_, 2nd ed., Artech
House, 2013. The primary reference for xref:frames.adoc[] and xref:gnss.adoc[]; equation numbers cited throughout
both pages (e.g., `(2.113)`, `(9.147)`) refer to this edition.

[[bib-groves-matlab]]
Groves, P. D., companion MATLAB scripts for the book above,
https://github.com/ymjdz/MATLAB-Codes[github.com/ymjdz/MATLAB-Codes]. Each converter/estimator class links to the
specific script it mirrors.

[[bib-borkowski1989]]
Borkowski, K. M., "Accurate algorithms to transform geocentric to geodetic coordinates," _Bulletin Géodésique_,
vol. 63, no. 1, pp. 50-56, 1989. Origin of the closed-form ECEF-to-geodetic solution referenced as Appendix C,
equations `(C.29)-(C.38)`, of <<bib-groves2013,Groves, 2013>> (that appendix ships only on the book's companion DVD, not in
the print/PDF edition; xref:frames.adoc[] documents the algorithm from this repository's own worked-out
implementation instead of the unavailable appendix text).

[[bib-karney2013]]
Karney, C. F. F., "Algorithms for geodesics," _Journal of Geodesy_, vol. 87, pp. 43-55, 2013,
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00190-012-0578-z[doi:10.1007/s00190-012-0578-z] (see also the
https://geographiclib.sourceforge.io/geod-addenda.html[addenda]). The core reference for xref:geodesic.adoc[].

[[bib-karney2011]]
Karney, C. F. F., "Geodesics on an ellipsoid of revolution," Feb. 2011,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.1215v1[arXiv:1102.1215v1].

[[bib-geographiclib]]
Karney, C. F. F., GeographicLib, https://geographiclib.sourceforge.io[geographiclib.sourceforge.io] (project
page: https://sourceforge.net/projects/geographiclib/[sourceforge.net/projects/geographiclib]). The
`com.irurueta.navigation.geodesic` package is a Java port of this library.

[[bib-shewchuk1997]]
Shewchuk, J. R., "Adaptive Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic and Fast Robust Geometric Predicates," _Discrete &
Computational Geometry_, vol. 18, no. 3, pp. 305-363, 1997,
https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00009321[doi:10.1007/PL00009321]. Basis for `Accumulator`'s extended-precision running
sum, used by `PolygonArea`.

[[bib-goldberg1991]]
Goldberg, D., "What Every Computer Scientist Should Know About Floating-Point Arithmetic," _ACM Computing
Surveys_, vol. 23, no. 1, pp. 5-48, 1991, https://doi.org/10.1145/103162.103163[doi:10.1145/103162.103163].
Basis (Theorem 4) for `GeoMath.log1p`.

[[bib-higham2002]]
Higham, N. J., _Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms_, 2nd ed., SIAM, 2002. Further reference for
`GeoMath.log1p`.

[[bib-moler1983]]
Moler, C., and D. Morrison, "Replacing Square Roots by Pythagorean Sums," _IBM Journal of Research and
Development_, vol. 27, no. 6, pp. 577-581, 1983, https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.276.0577[doi:10.1147/rd.276.0577].
Alternative method noted in `GeoMath.hypot`.

[[bib-dubrulle1983]]
Dubrulle, A. A., "A Class of Numerical Methods for the Computation of Pythagorean Sums," _IBM Journal of Research
and Development_, vol. 27, no. 6, pp. 582-589, 1983, https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.276.0582[doi:10.1147/rd.276.0582].
Alternative method noted in `GeoMath.hypot`.

[[bib-lemmingapex]]
lemmingapex, trilateration, https://github.com/lemmingapex/trilateration[github.com/lemmingapex/trilateration].
Base implementation referenced by the linear and non-linear solvers in xref:lateration.adoc[].

[[bib-hereman-murphy]]
Hereman, W., and W. S. Murphy Jr., "Determination of a Position in Three Dimensions Using Trilateration and
Approximate Distances." Background for the inhomogeneous-linear lateration solver in xref:lateration.adoc[].

[[bib-wikipedia-trilateration]]
Wikipedia contributors, "Trilateration," _Wikipedia, The Free Encyclopedia_,
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trilateration[en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trilateration]. General background on the
trilateration/multilateration/triangulation distinction used in the xref:lateration.adoc[] overview.

[[bib-gisgeography-trilateration]]
GIS Geography, "Trilateration vs Triangulation: How GPS Receivers Work,"
https://gisgeography.com/trilateration-triangulation-gps/[gisgeography.com/trilateration-triangulation-gps].
Accessible explanation of how GPS applies trilateration, referenced in the xref:lateration.adoc[] overview.
